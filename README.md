🌾 DIFMS – Digital Indian Farming & Monitoring System

NASA Space Apps Challenge | Team VARAHI AGRI

DIFMS is an AI and IoT-powered digital farming solution designed to support Indian farmers through NASA Earth observation data, ground-level sensor monitoring, and intelligent agricultural recommendations.

Our vision is to connect space technology with agriculture and help farmers make informed decisions from seed selection to harvesting and selling.

---

🌍 1. Problem Statement

Indian farmers face several challenges:

- Unpredictable weather and changing climate conditions.
- Difficulty in understanding soil and crop conditions.
- Limited access to reliable agricultural information.
- Uncertainty in selecting suitable crops.
- Difficulty accessing market information and government support.

DIFMS aims to bring important farming information together in one digital platform.

🚀 2. Our Solution

DIFMS integrates satellite-derived information, IoT sensor readings, and AI-assisted analysis to support smarter agricultural decisions.

Our system connects:

- NASA Earth observation data.
- Ground-level IoT monitoring.
- Farmer and field information.
- AI-assisted agricultural recommendations.
- Digital agricultural marketplace concepts.

🛰️ 3. NASA Data Integration

NASA Earth observation and environmental data are important components of DIFMS.

NASA POWER

We integrate NASA POWER data to obtain relevant environmental and meteorological information that can support agricultural analysis.

Official resources:

- NASA POWER: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/
- NASA POWER Documentation: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/

The application uses NASA data alongside ground-level information to provide farmers with a better understanding of field conditions.

🌱 4. IoT-Based Ground Monitoring

DIFMS incorporates an IoT monitoring concept using sensors and microcontrollers.

Proposed and prototype hardware components include:

- ESP8266 / ESP32
- DHT11 temperature and humidity sensor
- Soil moisture sensor
- Soil pH sensor
- ESP32-CAM

The collected ground-level readings can be compared with relevant environmental and satellite-derived information.

🤖 5. AI-Assisted Agricultural Analysis

DIFMS aims to provide intelligent agricultural assistance through:

- Crop selection support.
- Soil-condition-based recommendations.
- Agricultural suggestions based on available environmental information.
- Crop monitoring and disease-support concepts.
- Market-demand and crop-planning assistance.

📱 6. Application Features

- Farmer registration and login.
- Digital farmer and field identification.
- GPS-based field location information.
- Crop selection and crop dashboard.
- NASA environmental data integration.
- Ground-level IoT monitoring.
- Agricultural recommendations.
- Digital marketplace concept.
